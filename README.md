# 🏥 Insurance Policy Analysis

A data analytics project focused on identifying which customer segments make the most insurance claims — and understanding the reasons behind those patterns.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes real-world insurance data including customer profiles, policy types, and claim history. The goal was to uncover actionable insights about high-risk demographic groups and present them in a clear, visual format for business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Insurance companies often struggle to identify **which customer segments** are driving the most claims. Without this clarity, resources are misallocated and risk cannot be properly priced. This project solves that by:

- Identifying high-claim demographic groups
- Understanding the **why** behind claim patterns
- Presenting findings in an easy-to-understand dashboard

---

---

## 📊 Dashboard & KPIs

The Power BI dashboard includes the following Key Performance Indicators:

- **Total Claims** — overall volume of claims filed
- **Claim Rate by Age Group** — which age brackets claim most
- **Claim Rate by Policy Type** — which policies have highest risk
- **Top 5 High-Claim Segments** — demographic breakdown
- **Average Claim Value** — financial impact per claim

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Identified specific demographic groups responsible for the highest claim volumes
- Discovered patterns linking policy type and customer age to claim frequency
- Presented clean, visual conclusions that were well-received in a batch presentation at ExcelR
